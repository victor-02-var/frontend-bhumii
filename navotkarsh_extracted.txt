CleanRoute -Smart Waste Management
Navotkarsh
Platform
Idea/Solution:
CleanRoute is an integrated smart waste-management platform that
connects citizens, municipal authorities, waste vehicles, and collection
points on a single system. It combines real-time GPS fleet tracking,
intelligent route optimization, bin monitoring, priority-based complaint
management, and alerts to enable faster, more efficient, and
accountable waste collection.
Key Components :
GPS Vehicle Tracking: Real-time visibility of garbage-truck locations, route
progress and fleet activity.
AI-Powered Route Optimization: Generates efficient daily routes based on
collection points, vehicle capacity and operational constraints.
Bin + Overflow Monitoring: Tracks fill levels and automatically prioritizes
high-fill bins for collection.
End-to-End Complaint Management: Citizens report issues, receive a ticket,
and track resolution while administrators assign and monitor actions.
